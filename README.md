### Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn patterns using customer subscription data.

The objective is to understand why customers leave, identify customer groups with higher churn, analyze customer lifetime patterns and cohorts, and provide practical recommendations for improving customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer churn and retention rates
- Identify major reasons for customer churn
- Compare churn across subscription plans
- Analyze churn across regions and customer segments
- Study customer cohorts
- Analyze customer tenure and lifetime value
- Identify actionable retention opportunities
- Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains customer subscription and behavioral information, including:

- Customer ID

## 💡 Key Insights

- The overall customer churn rate is 23.40%.
- 234 customers are recorded as churned, while 766 customers remain active.
- "Price too high" is the largest recorded reason for customer churn.
- "Poor service" and "Switched competitor" are also major recorded churn reasons.
- Churn varies across subscription plans, regions, customer segments and cohorts.
- Customer lifetime and tenure analysis can help identify retention opportunities.
- The Individual customer segment shows a higher observed churn rate than the Enterprise and Small Business segments.

---

## 💼 Recommendations

### 1. Review Pricing and Plan Value
Review pricing, discounts and plan benefits because price-related concerns are the largest recorded churn reason.

### 2. Improve Service Quality
Strengthen customer support and resolve service-related issues more quickly.

### 3. Focus on High-Churn Customer Segments
Develop targeted retention campaigns for customer groups showing higher observed churn.

### 4. Strengthen Technical Support
Investigate recurring technical problems and improve troubleshooting and customer assistance.

### 5. Improve Early Customer Engagement
Use cohort and tenure analysis to identify periods when customers are more likely to leave and strengthen onboarding and engagement during those periods.
